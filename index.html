Friday hike: White Banks Trail, Elk Neck State Park

GPS

Search "Rogues Harbor Boat Ramp, North East, MD" in Google or Apple Maps. If that doesn't resolve, use the park headquarters, 4395 Turkey Point Rd, North East, MD 21901, then continue on Route 272 South for 1 mile and turn left onto Rogues Harbor Road. The lot is at the end.

Park office: (410) 287-5333 · Elk Neck duty ranger line not published; use the office number.

Driving from I-95

I-95 North to exit 100 (Route 272, North East). Go south on 272 through the town of North East. Stay on 272 about 9 miles to the park entrance and headquarters, then 1 more mile and left on Rogues Harbor Road. About 20 minutes from the interstate. The Turkey Point Lighthouse lot is 3 miles farther, at the very end of 272.

The trail

3 miles one way · park rates it Difficult for hiking · steep hills, tight turns, cliffs

From the Rogues Harbor lot, follow the White Banks Trail toward the white clay cliffs, about 100 feet above the Chesapeake. Hike out to the cliffs and back for roughly 2 to 3 miles. The cliff overlook faces west over the bay, so time it for sunset. Stay back from the cliff edges; the clay is soft and undercut.

The trail also starts from the North East Beach Area near Shelter 1, farther north on 272, if the Rogues Harbor lot is closed. The boat-launch piers were under construction through February 2026, so a lingering closure is possible.

Optional add-on: Beaver Marsh Loop

2 miles · Difficult · starts at the same Rogues Harbor lot · waterfront section floods at high tide

Links to the southern end of White Banks. Tonight's tide: low was 3:10 PM, high is 9:21 PM at the Chesapeake City gauge, so the water is rising all evening. If you want the marsh, do it first, right when you arrive. After about 6 PM expect the shoreline stretch to be wet or under water.

Timing and light

Sunset 6:12 PM, civil dusk about 6:40. Be at the cliffs by 6:00. Under trees it is dark by 6:30, so bring phone lights or headlamps for the walk back. Clear and about 60°F this afternoon, low 50s on the water by sunset. Portable toilets at the Turkey Point lot; none confirmed at Rogues Harbor.

After the hike: Rogues Harbor to Elkton Lodge is about 25 minutes. Back up 272 to North East, then Route 40 East or I-95 North one exit to Elkton. Call the lodge at (410) 398-9400 if you'll arrive after 9 PM.

Hiking

Fair Hill Natural Resources Management Area, Elkton

10 min from the Elkton hotels · HQ at 300 Tawes Drive, Elkton MD 21921

5,600 acres and 80-plus miles of trails. The Orange Trail is a 5.8-mile loop with some climbs, about two hours. Park at any of the five day-use lots. Horses have right of way, and late October foliage here is the best in the area. Trail map (PDF)

White Clay Creek State Park, Newark

Wraps around the north side of campus · day-use fee about $5 to $10 for out-of-state plates

The Carpenter Recreation Area off Route 896 is the main trailhead, and the creek-side trails from the Chambers House lot on Creek Road are the prettiest easy walk. Park page

Turkey Point Lighthouse, Elk Neck State Park, North East

About 30 min south of Elkton · end of Route 272 · sunrise to sunset

An easy 1 to 2 mile round trip on crushed stone to an 1833 lighthouse on a bluff over the Chesapeake. The lot fills on weekends, so go before 10 a.m., or go for sunset. The lighthouse is climbable on weekends through November. Lighthouse page

Biking

Michael Castle Trail, C&D Canal

12.4 mi paved, car-free · Chesapeake City, MD to Delaware City, DE

Flat and scenic, with ships going by. Start at the Chesapeake City lot on the north side of the canal and ride as far as you like. Trail details

Middle Run Natural Area and White Clay Creek, Newark

Free lot on Possum Hollow Road near Tri-State Bird Rescue · fills quickly on a dry day

Smooth, fast, hard-packed singletrack rated mostly intermediate, with a skills area. Skip it if the trails are wet. Rider reviews

Fair Hill, Elkton

Best beginner-friendly off-road riding nearby

The Red Trail is an easy 2.5-mile loop on an old logging road, and the Blue Diamond Trail is 6.8 miles of gravel double-track that holds up after rain. Bikes yield to hikers, and everyone yields to horses.

Something else

Milburn Orchards, Elkton

1495 Appleton Road, Elkton MD · 5 min from Elkton Lodge · Sat 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., Sun 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., last entry one hour before close, weather permitting

This weekend, October 24 and 25, is one of their Peak Fall Festival weekends. Apple and pumpkin picking, cider donuts, corn maze, and the Big BackYard play area. Fall admission tickets

Chesapeake City, MD

15 min from Elkton · walkable historic canal town

Pair it with the canal bike ride, then eat on the water at Bayard House (11 Bohemia Ave), known for crab soup and crab cakes, or Schaefer's Canal House (208 Bank St) across the canal with a deck bar. The C&D Canal Museum with its giant 1829 water wheel is free, but check weekend hours since one listing says weekdays only. Town guide

Go Ape at Lums Pond State Park, Bear DE

About 20 min from Newark · seasonal, book online first

Zipline and treetop ropes course. Lums Pond also rents kayaks and has a flat 7-mile loop trail around the pond. Go Ape booking

Main Street, Newark

A few blocks from campus and the Hyatt Place

For the evening: Iron Hill Brewery, Deer Park Tavern, and a dozen other spots within walking distance.
